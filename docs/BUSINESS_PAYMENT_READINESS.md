# Business And Payment Readiness

This is an operating checklist, not legal or tax advice.

## Current Safe Mode

Okay before forming an LLC or enabling payment:

- Discovery outreach.
- Demo conversations.
- Unpaid 14-day pilots.
- Email-first lead alerts.
- Manual setup for one approved capture workflow.
- Tracking prospects, leads, pilot requests, and outcomes.

Avoid before business/payment setup:

- Charging recurring subscriptions.
- Promising production SMS.
- Signing long customer commitments.
- Handling sensitive customer data beyond basic lead intake.
- Presenting the product as a fully self-serve SaaS.

## Before Charging Customers

Do these before collecting payment:

- Decide business structure, likely LLC if continuing commercially.
- Get tax/accounting advice if unsure.
- Set up a business bank account or separate payment account.
- Set up Stripe in production.
- Add Terms of Service.
- Add Privacy Policy.
- Add refund/cancellation language.
- Decide support email and response expectations.
- Make sure outbound claims match current capabilities.

## Suggested Initial Paid Offer

Only after the above is ready:

- 14-day pilot.
- Then $79/month for Lead Text-Back.
- Cancel anytime.
- One business/location.
- One approved lead source.
- One approved reply template.
- SMS only if Twilio readiness is complete.

## Minimum Terms To Cover

The eventual terms/privacy docs should cover:

- What LocalLeadReply does and does not automate.
- Customer responsibility for approved wording.
- No guaranteed lead volume or booked jobs.
- Data collected from lead forms.
- Email notifications and dashboard access.
- SMS consent and opt-out handling once SMS exists.
- Cancellation and refund policy.
- Limitation of liability.
